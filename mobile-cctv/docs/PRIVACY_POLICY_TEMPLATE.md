# Privacy Policy Template (starting point — have this reviewed)

**DigiforgeDynamics CCTV** ("the App") lets you use one device's camera and
microphone to stream live video to another device you control, using an
end-to-end WebRTC connection.

- **What we collect:** The signaling server temporarily stores a pairing
  code used only to connect your two devices. It does not store, view, or
  have access to your video or audio stream — media flows directly between
  your devices.
- **What we don't do:** We do not sell, share, or use your video/audio for
  any purpose. We do not enable remote access by anyone other than the
  viewer device you pair.
- **Permissions used:** Camera, microphone, and network access, used only to
  capture and transmit the live stream between your own paired devices.
- **Data retention:** Pairing codes expire automatically within an hour if
  unused and are deleted after use.
- **Consent:** Only use this app to record spaces and people where you have
  the legal right and consent to do so under your local laws.

Replace this with your own reviewed policy before publishing — requirements
vary by region (GDPR in the EU, DPDP in India, etc.) and by store.
